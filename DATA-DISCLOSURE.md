# Data and Artifact Disclosure

The public repository exposes the evaluation scripts, model documentation,
schemas, aggregate result artifacts, and reproducibility pointers needed to
understand the analysis.

This public-submission directory does not include raw historical player-
comparison archives, raw source CSVs, restricted upstream feeds, private
generated inputs, or operational data. Those materials remain subject to their
original licenses and access controls.

The aggregate result tables here are sufficient to inspect the submission
claim. The repository-level scripts identify the source artifacts required for
an authorized reproduction without publishing restricted data.
